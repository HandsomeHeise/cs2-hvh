The Windows Arial font is loaded from the local Windows installation when
available; the font is not redistributed. UI geometry is drawn with ImGui.
The user's reference image is not bundled into or rendered by the DLL.

Item names, models and cosmetic compatibility metadata are derived from the
user's installed CS2 VPK files by tools/import_weapon_names.py and
tools/import_cosmetics.py. Source hashes and extraction coverage are recorded
in analysis/weapon-names-source.json and analysis/cosmetics-source.json.
Full game assets, inventory icons and textures are not redistributed.

Compiled model body choices are read from the installed resources by
tools/import_cosmetic_geometry.py. Its bounded binary KV3 decoder follows
the public ValveResourceFormat layout documentation/source at this commit:
https://github.com/ValveResourceFormat/ValveResourceFormat/tree/0d72f09ac9f36cbc4eae04b8541defeba1802ff5
The exact format references and model hashes are in
analysis/cosmetic-legacy-assets.json. Model meshes and materials are not bundled.
